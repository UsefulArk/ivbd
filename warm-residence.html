COME ON IN

A closer look.
Room by room.

Explore the spaces, materials and small decisions that bring the whole apartment together. Select any image for a larger view.

01 / Living room

A living room to come home to

An ivory L-shaped sofa and a rounded walnut table anchor the living zone. A freestanding TV console divides it from dining while keeping the connection open, with sliding glazing to the balcony.

02 / Dining

Room for everyone at the table

A six-seat walnut table sits in the open dining zone beside the living room. The table’s arrangement keeps routes to the bedrooms and kitchen clear.

03 / Kitchen

Beautifully organised, every day

Walnut cabinetry runs along two walls, with the sink on the rear counter, the hob and fridge along the side, and an open centre. Separate doors connect the kitchen to dining and the laundry room.

04 / Primary bedroom

A quieter end to the day

The bed occupies the quieter side of the room, with wall storage and clear access to the private en suite in the corner.

05 / Guest bedroom

Make your guests feel at home

A comfortable double bed and wall-fitted wardrobe follow the guest-room layout. Clear space at the foot of the bed leads to the southeast entrance.

06 / Bedroom & study

A room that can change with you

A single bed, a wardrobe and a desk fitted against the bedroom wall make a flexible third room. The desk and chair remain inside the bedroom, with the entrance and central floor clear.

07 / Bathroom

A considered everyday ritual

The shared bathroom is reached from the main circulation area. A compact vanity, toilet with handheld bidet and a glass shower are arranged around clear dry standing space.

08 / Balcony

Your evening tea, with a view

Two woven chairs and a small round table sit together at one end of the covered balcony. The living-room slider and its approach stay clear.

09 / En suite

A private space to refresh

The private en suite connects directly to the primary bedroom. A compact vanity, toilet and separate shower follow the approved corner layout.

10 / Utility & laundry

The practical side, considered too

A washing machine and dryer sit side by side along the laundry wall, with a utility sink at the end. Access is directly from the kitchen, with the doorway and middle of the room clear.
